Primary Research Question

"How does increasing label noise affect category-learning accuracy and generalization in college students compared with neural networks?"

Stream A: Clean Data Baseline (0% Noise)

Deterministic

Under noise-free deterministic category structures, both humans and neural networks converge smoothly toward high categorization accuracy.

Human BenchmarkHigh GeneralizationRapid rule abstraction
Neural Net (MLP/ResNet)Smooth OptimizationGradient descent convergence

Stream B: Injected Noise (20% – 40%)

Stochastic

When feedback contains stochastic label errors, human participants exhibit robust heuristic filtering, while standard neural nets memorize contradictions.

Human ResilienceHeuristic FilteringIgnores sporadic anomalies
Neural OverfittingMemorization DecayDistorted decision manifolds

Key Empirical Takeaway

Humans utilize inductive rule filters that discard stochastic training noise as statistical outliers. Standard gradient optimization without explicit regularization penalizes training error on noisy targets, distorting the classification manifold.
